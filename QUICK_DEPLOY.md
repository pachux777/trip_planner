## ⚡ One-Click Deploy

### Vercel (Recommended)
1. Go to: https://vercel.com/new
2. Connect GitHub repository
3. Click "Deploy"
4. Your site will be live in 2-3 minutes!

### Netlify
1. Go to: https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Drag your `build` folder
3. Your site will be live instantly!
